Briefing
Your mission: find out which living sorcerers at Jujutsu High can really perform the Reverse Cursed Technique (RCT), and prove it with data.

Six months ago a curse called the Inverter got into the Jujutsu Records Office. Since then, the infirmary has recorded sorcerers recovering from critical injuries impossibly fast, without a healer. That is the signature of RCT: turning negative cursed energy into positive energy to heal yourself.

The Principal needs the true list before the next Special Grade incident. But the Records Office warns that the Inverter doesn't just hide things: it creates miracles that never happened. Every number you use, you'll have to defend.
